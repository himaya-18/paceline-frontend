PACELINE / ABOUT

Our Story

We opened Paceline in 2015 with one goal — to build the running shop we'd always wanted. A decade in, we're still hand-picking every shoe, every layer and every accessory for runners who take their running seriously.

01 · WHY WE EXIST

We're all
about the run.

Running is more than a sport at Paceline. It is how we challenge ourselves, clear our heads and connect with the people around us.

We believe the right equipment should never get in the way of that feeling. That is why we search for products that combine thoughtful design, real performance and everyday comfort.

From the first kilometre to race day, Paceline exists to help runners find their next reason to keep moving.

— Rae & Jamie, Founders
02 · WHAT WE STAND FOR

Three things we won't compromise on.

01

Curated, not sold to us.

Every product earns its place on our shelves through performance, comfort and genuine runner feedback.

02

Community over transaction.

We want runners to feel part of something bigger than a shopping trip. Advice comes first. The sale comes second.

03

Craft in every detail.

From gait analysis to the final product recommendation, we care about getting the details right.

03 · THE SEASONS IN

How we got here.

2015

Doors open.

Paceline opens its first Glasgow store with a simple idea: make running equipment easier to choose.

2018

The clinic launches.

Gait analysis becomes part of our service, helping runners understand how their equipment works with them.

2022

Weekly run club.

Our community begins meeting every week for shared miles, advice and conversation.

2026

40 brands & counting.

A decade later, Paceline continues to search for better products and better ways to support runners.
